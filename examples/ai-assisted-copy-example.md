# AI-Assisted Copy Example

This example shows how AI can support copywriting while keeping human review in the process.

## Original Rough Brief

Create a landing page hero for a fictional industrial construction campaign. The message should be about planning, reliability, and helping teams feel confident before site work begins. It should sound modern and professional, not too dramatic.

## Prompt Used

```text
Act as a copy support partner for a digital campaign.

Create 5 hero headline options, 3 short paragraph options, and 5 CTA labels for a fictional industrial construction campaign.

Audience:
Operations, procurement, and project leads at industrial companies.

Tone:
Professional, direct, reliable, modern.

Message:
Good planning helps teams reduce confusion before construction begins.

Rules:
- Do not mention real companies.
- Do not include fake statistics.
- Do not overpromise results.
- Keep the copy suitable for a landing page hero.
```

## AI-Generated Draft

**Headline options**

1. Build with confidence from day one.
2. Stronger planning for complex construction.
3. Move from concept to site with less confusion.
4. Industrial construction starts with clear coordination.
5. Plan the work before the work begins.

**Paragraph option**

Support your next industrial construction project with clearer planning, better coordination, and a practical path from early decisions to site delivery.

**CTA options**

- Start planning
- Talk to the team
- Plan the next phase
- Review the process
- Build with clarity

## Human-Edited Final Version

**Headline**  
Build with clarity before the first beam goes up.

**Paragraph**  
Planning-led construction support for teams that need safer timelines, cleaner coordination, and confidence from concept to site delivery.

**CTA**  
Plan the next phase

**Feature list**

- Project planning aligned before site work begins
- Clear coordination between field, design, and delivery teams
- Practical reporting for safer decisions and smoother handoff

## Why the Human Edits Improved It

- The final headline is more specific and more memorable than the generic draft options.
- The phrase "before the first beam goes up" adds campaign character while staying relevant to construction.
- The paragraph keeps the AI draft structure but makes the benefit clearer.
- The CTA is direct without feeling too aggressive.
- The feature list makes the section easier to scan.
- The final copy avoids fake statistics, unrealistic claims, and real client references.
